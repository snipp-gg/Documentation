---
title: "Get Analytics"
openapi: "api-reference/openapi.json GET /analytics"
---

Fetch your analytics: posts, uploads, data uploaded, views, comments and likes received, and followers gained, over time and in total. This is the data behind the Analytics page in the Snipp Console.

## Behavior

- Requires Snipp+ or Ultra. Free accounts get `403` with type `plan_required`.
- `range` sets the window for `series` and `deltas`: `7`, `30`, `90` or `365` days. Omitted means `30`; any other value returns `400`.
- `buckets` are always today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days, whatever `range` is.
- `series` has one entry per day across the window, oldest first, including days with a value of `0`.
- `totals.posts` and `totals.views` are lifetime. `totals.comments`, `totals.likes` and `totals.bytes` cover the fetch window, which is twice `range` with a floor of 30 days.
- `deltas.change` is the fractional change against the previous window of the same length. It is `null` when the previous window was `0` and the current one is not, and `0` when both are `0`.
- All days are UTC.

## Examples

```bash
curl "https://api.snipp.gg/analytics?range=90" \
  -H "api-key: YOUR_API_KEY"
```

## Errors

| Status | Type | When |
|--------|------|------|
| `400` | `invalid_request` | `range` is not 7, 30, 90 or 365 |
| `401` | `unauthorized` | Missing or invalid key |
| `403` | `plan_required` | Your plan does not include analytics |
| `403` | `suspended` | Your account is suspended |
| `429` | `rate_limited` | More than 30 requests per minute |
