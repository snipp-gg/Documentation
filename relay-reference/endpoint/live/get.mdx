---
title: "Get Your Live Stream"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json GET /live"
---

Read your live stream setup: the RTMPS server URL and stream key for OBS, whether you are live, your monthly live minutes and your chat moderators.

## Behavior

- Streaming needs Plus or Ultra. Free accounts get `403` with type `plan_required`.
- `live_stream` is `null` until you [set up streaming](/relay-reference/endpoint/live/create).
- `ingest_url` is `rtmps://live.snipp.gg:443/live/`. Paste it and `stream_key` into OBS (Settings, Stream, Custom). Keep `stream_key` secret: anyone with it can stream as you.
- `ingest_url` and `stream_key` are `null` if the stream service could not be reached; try again.
- You go live automatically when your encoder connects. `live`, `started_at` and `viewers` reflect the current broadcast; `viewers` refreshes about once a minute.
- `minutes` is your monthly live streaming budget in whole minutes: 100 on Plus, 300 on Ultra. Minutes only count while you are live and reset together with [priority minutes](/concepts/plans#priority-minutes). When they run out the stream ends.
- `moderators` lists the users who can remove messages, time people out and ban people in your chat.
- `chat_enabled` and `chat_slow_seconds` are your chat settings. Change them with [Update Live Stream](/relay-reference/endpoint/live/update).
- Team keys are not accepted and get `401`.

## Example

```bash
curl "https://relay.snipp.gg/live" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```
