---
title: "Update Live Stream"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json PATCH /live"
---

Change your stream title or replace your list of chat moderators.

## Behavior

- Send `title`, `moderators` or both. Sending neither is `400`.
- `title` is up to 140 characters. An empty string or `null` clears it, and the watch page shows a default title instead.
- `moderators` is the complete list of user IDs, up to 10, and replaces the current list. Send `[]` to remove everyone. You cannot add yourself. A user who does not exist or is suspended gives `404`, and a user who blocked you, or whom you blocked, gives `403` with type `blocked`.
- Moderators can remove messages and [time people out](/relay-reference/endpoint/live/timeout) in your chat. Only you and staff can time out a moderator.
- Returns `404` if streaming is not set up.

## Example

```bash
curl -X PATCH "https://relay.snipp.gg/live" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"title": "Ranked grind", "moderators": ["987654321098765432"]}'
```
