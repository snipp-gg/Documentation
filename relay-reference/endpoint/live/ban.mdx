---
title: "Ban Chat User"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json POST /users/{id}/live/bans/{user_id}"
---

Stop a user from chatting in a stream until they are unbanned. Their messages in that chat are removed. They can still watch.

## Behavior

- Only the streamer, their moderators and staff can ban people; anyone else gets `403` with type `forbidden`.
- Banning the streamer gives `403` with type `forbidden`, and so does banning a moderator unless you are the streamer or staff. Banning yourself is `400`.
- Banning someone who is timed out replaces the timeout. Banning someone again refreshes their `banned_at`.
- While banned, [sending a message](/relay-reference/endpoint/live/chat-send) returns `403` with type `banned`.
- The streamer can list everyone banned with [List Banned Chat Users](/relay-reference/endpoint/live/bans).

## Example

```bash
curl -X POST "https://relay.snipp.gg/users/987654321098765432/live/bans/123456789012345678" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```
