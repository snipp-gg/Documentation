---
title: "List Banned Chat Users"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json GET /live/bans"
---

List the people banned from your stream's chat, newest ban first.

## Behavior

- Needs Plus or Ultra, like the other endpoints for your own stream; otherwise `403` with type `plan_required`.
- Paginated with `limit` and `cursor`. See [Pagination](/concepts/pagination).
- Timeouts are not listed, only bans.

## Example

```bash
curl "https://relay.snipp.gg/live/bans?limit=20" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```
