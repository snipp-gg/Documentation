title: "Get Upload"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json GET /upload/{id}"
---

Status of an upload sent in pieces (see [Large Files](/relay-reference/endpoint/upload#large-files)), then its result.

## Behavior

- While pieces are still arriving, `200` with `upload.status: uploading`. After the last piece, `upload.status: processing` until the file is stored.
- When processing finishes, `200` with the same `{url, post}` body `POST /upload` returns for one file, or the `{post}` body of `POST /posts/{code}/files` when the pieces went there. A failed upload returns its error at the status that endpoint would have used.
- The result is kept for 10 minutes after processing finishes. After that, and for an id that is unknown, expired or belongs to another account or team, the response is `404`.
- Send it to `https://{region}.relay.snipp.gg` from `upload.region`.
- 20 requests per 30 seconds. Polling every 2 seconds stays under it.

## Example

```bash
curl "https://eu-west-1.relay.snipp.gg/upload/9f86d081884c7d659a2feaa0c55ad015" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```
