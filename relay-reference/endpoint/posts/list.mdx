---
title: "List Posts"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json GET /posts"
---

List your own posts, newest first.

## Behavior

- Returns every personal post you own, whatever its privacy, including moderated and restricted ones. Drafts and team posts are not included.
- Results are [paginated](/concepts/pagination): pass `limit` (1 to 100, default 30) and the previous page's `next_cursor` as `cursor`.
- Each item is a full post object, with `moderated` and `restricted` since you own it.
- File URLs on private posts are signed and expire after 24 hours. List again for fresh ones.
- Team keys are not accepted here and get `401`.

## Examples

```bash
curl "https://relay.snipp.gg/posts?limit=50" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```

## Errors

| Status | Type | When |
|--------|------|------|
| `400` | `invalid_request` | Invalid `limit` or `cursor` |
| `401` | `unauthorized` | Missing or invalid key |
| `403` | `suspended` | Your account is suspended |
| `429` | `rate_limited` | More than 20 requests in 30 seconds |
