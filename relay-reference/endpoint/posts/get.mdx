---
title: "Get Post"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json GET /posts/{code}"
---

Fetch a post by its share code.

## Behavior

- `files` lists every file in the post, in order. `file_count` is its length; a post with more than one file is an album.
- `url` is the share page. Each `files[].url` is the direct file. On private posts the file and thumbnail URLs are signed and expire after 24 hours; fetch the post again for fresh ones.
- `files[].name` is what [Delete File](/relay-reference/endpoint/posts/delete-file) takes.
- `files[].thumbnail_url` is a still frame for videos uploaded with [`POST /upload`](/relay-reference/endpoint/upload), and `null` otherwise. `width`, `height`, `size` and `mime_type` are `null` when unknown.
- `liked` is whether you have liked the post.
- `moderated` and `restricted` are only present when you own the post. A restricted post keeps its privacy, is hidden from Discover and profiles, and cannot be made public.
- When you own a personal post, it also carries `access`: the users who can open it while it is private. Change it with [Update Post](/relay-reference/endpoint/posts/update).
- Each request counts a view on public posts that are not moderated. Views are deduplicated per IP and post: up to 3 counted views per IP per post per hour. The returned `view_count` includes the view just counted.
- **Who can see what:**
  - Drafts are visible only to their owner.
  - Private posts are visible to their owner and to users the owner has given access to.
  - Team posts are visible only to members of that team. They have `team_id` set and `like_count` and `liked` are `null`, since team posts cannot be liked.
  - Posts whose owner is suspended are visible only to the owner.
  - Anyone else gets `404` with type `not_found`.
- A moderated post is returned in full to its owner, with `moderated: true`. Anyone else who could otherwise see it gets `403` with type `moderated`.
- If the post's author has blocked you, the response is `403` with type `blocked`.
- Shares a rate limit bucket with [`GET /users/{id}/posts`](/relay-reference/endpoint/users/posts).

## Examples

```bash
curl "https://relay.snipp.gg/posts/AbCd1234" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```

## Errors

| Status | Type | When |
|--------|------|------|
| `400` | `invalid_request` | `code` is not a valid share code |
| `401` | `unauthorized` | Missing or invalid key, or a team key |
| `403` | `moderated` | The post is moderated and you are not its owner |
| `403` | `blocked` | The author has blocked you |
| `404` | `not_found` | No such post, or you cannot see it |
| `429` | `rate_limited` | More than 20 requests in 30 seconds |
