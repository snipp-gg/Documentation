---
title: "Remove Avatar"
openapi: "relay-reference/openapi.json DELETE /users/{id}/avatar"
---

Remove your avatar. `id` must be `@me` or your own ID.

## Behavior

- Deletes the stored avatar. Your `avatar` falls back to `https://cdn.snipp.gg/avatar.png`.
- Removing a avatar you do not have succeeds.
- The response is your full account.
- Shares a rate limit bucket with [Upload Avatar](/relay-reference/endpoint/users/upload-avatar): 10 requests per minute.

## Examples

```bash
curl -X DELETE "https://relay.snipp.gg/users/@me/avatar" \
  -H "relay-key: YOUR_RELAY_KEY"
```

## Errors

| Status | Type | When |
|--------|------|------|
| `401` | `unauthorized` | Missing or invalid key |
| `403` | `forbidden` | `id` is not you |
| `429` | `rate_limited` | More than 10 requests per minute |
